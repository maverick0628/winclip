VERIFICATION
Verification is intended to assist the Chocolatey moderators and community
in verifying that this package's contents are trustworthy.

This package downloads the official WinClip installer at install time. It is
not repackaged, and no binary ships inside the package.

1. Download the installer from the official WinClip website:

   x64:   https://duncansmith.tech/apps/winclip/releases/v2.2.0/WinClip-2.2.0-x64-Setup.exe
   arm64: https://duncansmith.tech/apps/winclip/releases/v2.2.0/WinClip-2.2.0-arm64-Setup.exe

2. Get the SHA256 checksum with either:
   - the PowerShell function 'Get-FileHash'
   - the Chocolatey utility 'checksum.exe'

3. The checksums must match these. They are what tools\chocolateyinstall.ps1
   verifies against, and what SHA256SUMS.txt beside the installers publishes:

   WinClip-2.2.0-x64-Setup.exe
   SHA256: 730F24B89F251663CD09C9DF8A13774A253AE27961E5F22FC8B2C3C8767DBDF8

   WinClip-2.2.0-arm64-Setup.exe
   SHA256: 2BF03F18DEB43623C76B69709F54A86034353E89DBC74644AC20EE805A92C6AE

4. The installers are signed. Check the signature with:

   (Get-AuthenticodeSignature .\WinClip-2.2.0-x64-Setup.exe).SignerCertificate.Subject

   It reads CN="DST Digital, LLC", O="DST Digital, LLC", L=Atlanta, S=Georgia, C=US.
